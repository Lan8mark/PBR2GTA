# Third-party notices

The packaged local worker includes Python, NumPy and OpenCV. Their licenses and
bundled third-party notices are included in `third_party_licenses/`. The release
build verifies that these files are present. Python's Windows distribution
license includes notices for its incorporated software.

The executable is built with PyInstaller. Its application-bundling exception
permits redistribution under the application's license; the licenses of the
included dependencies still apply.

NVIDIA Texture Tools is an external user-installed dependency and is not
distributed with PBR2GTA.
